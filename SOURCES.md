## Mechanism research

https://www.timezone.com/2002/10/15/an-explanation-of-the-gear-train-of-a-watch-1197/
Walt Odets, TimeZone archive (2002, quoting 1997): barrel, wheel/pinion train, escapement and balance power flow. Used for explanatory structure, not exact source-watch ratios.

https://www.hodinkee.com/articles/the-modern-watch-escapement-and-how-it-got-that-way
Hodinkee, 21 August 2020: balance, hairspring, pallet fork and escapement interaction. Motion in the study remains illustrative.

https://watchesbysjx.com/2024/09/chronograph-vertical-clutch-explained.html
SJX Watches, September 2024: chronograph vertical-clutch explanation. Used for conceptual timing-state separation, not an exact calibre layout.
